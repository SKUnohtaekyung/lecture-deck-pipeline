## 공통 최종 프롬프트 계약 (1주차 verbatim 인용)

```text
Use case: productivity-visual
Asset type: lecture slide explanatory or mnemonic support, transparent PNG source
Style/medium: VIBECODING PAPER-CUT v1, thick fine-fiber paper, crisp die-cut edges,
shallow layered relief, restrained internal contact shadows only where paper layers overlap,
modular calm geometry. Color palette: cool paper white, royal blue, pale blue, teal mint,
sparse coral only when semantically essential, near-black structural anchors.
Constraints: no people unless semantically required, no letters, readable text, numbers, logos,
UI frames, screenshots, Windows/Codex depiction, watermark, gradients, photo, brains, or circuit motifs.
OUTPUT CONTRACT: Render on one perfectly uniform solid magenta #FF00FF background with identical
RGB values: no texture, gradient, lighting, reflection, floor, horizon, vignette, or shadow outside
the object. Keep object away from canvas edges; do not use magenta in the object.
```

생성 뒤 처리 절차는 1주차와 동일: `remove_chroma_key.py --auto-key border --soft-matte --transparent-threshold 12 --opaque-threshold 220 --despill`로 균일 마젠타 배경을 제거하고, 필요하면 `prepare_image_asset.py --mode native-alpha --padding-ratio 0.05`로 여백을 보정한다. 최종 PNG는 RGBA·투명 모서리 4/4·마젠타 fringe 0을 확인한 뒤 `자료/images/`에 저장한다.
